Post 4 — reading tip

Quick reading win for home: After a page, ask “What just happened?” — not a quiz, just a conversation. If your child can retell it in their own words, comprehension is taking root. If retelling feels hard, that’s useful information, not a verdict. Fluency and comprehension are skills we can coach. K–5 reading support · $40/hour · learninggrove.org
