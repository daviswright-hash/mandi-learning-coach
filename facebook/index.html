1. Create the Page

  1. Open facebook.com/pages/create while signed in as Mandi (not Davis).
  2. Choose Business or brand.
  3. Paste the Page name, then category Private tutor (search “tutor” if you don’t see it).
  4. Paste the short bio.
  5. Upload profile-photo.jpg (circle) and cover-photo.jpg (banner).
  6. Add phone, email, website, and city from the contact block. Turn on messaging.
  7. Action button: Send message (this is what the ads use). Add Call as a second action if Facebook offers it.
  8. Paste the Messenger greeting. Publish the first three posts. Pin post 1.
