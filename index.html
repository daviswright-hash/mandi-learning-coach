Meet Mandi

Amanda Thurmond, M.Ed.

Mandi is a licensed teacher with a master’s degree in education and 18 years in the classroom. She has served as a literacy lead, curriculum developer, and intervention teacher — the person schools trust when a child needs a clearer path.

Now she brings that same care to families in Hixson and the greater Chattanooga area. Warm, practical, and relentlessly encouraging, she coaches K–5 students in reading, math, writing, homework habits, and test prep.

“Every child can learn. My job is to help them believe it.”
