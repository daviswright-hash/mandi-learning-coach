Questions

Straight answers

Where do sessions happen?

Only at Mandi’s house in Red Bank, Tennessee. The street address is sent when you book. It is not listed on this site.

How much does it cost?

$40 per hour, one-on-one. No packages. You pay for the time you book.

What grades do you work with?

Kindergarten through 5th grade. Reading, math, writing, homework and study skills, test prep, and enrichment for accelerated students.

Do you work with accelerated students?

Yes. If classroom work is already easy, we stretch: deeper reading, richer math, stronger writing — not more of the same worksheet. Same $40/hour, same Red Bank house, still K–5.

What should we bring?

Your child. A current book, a math page, a teacher note — whether they’re stuck or already ahead. Mandi has materials either way.

Can we text instead of email?

Yes. Text (929) 256-3772. That’s a Google Voice number — calls and texts both reach Mandi.
