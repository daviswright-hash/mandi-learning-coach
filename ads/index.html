How to launch in 45 minutes

  1. Create the Facebook Page (use the kit in ../facebook/).
  2. Meta Ads Manager → Create → Sales or Leads → Messages + Calls.
  3. One campaign, three ad sets only if budget ≥ $30/day. Otherwise one ad set, three ads (A, B, C) and let Meta pick.
  4. Upload the photos. Paste copy A/B/C. CTA: Send message. Pixel: skip for now.
  5. Radius 10 miles, parents 28–48.
  6. Nextdoor post the same day. Group posts the next morning.
  7. Google Search only after Meta is live, if you still have budget.
  8. After 5 days: turn off any ad with CTR under ~1% or zero messages. Keep the winner. Make two new versions of the winner.

Rules that protect results

  • Always show the price. $40/hour filters tire-kickers and beats chains.
  • Always show her face. Local services convert on a real adult.
  • Always offer text. Parents will not fill a long form at 9pm.
  • Never fake reviews or “limited spots” unless it’s true.
  • Never put the street address on the ad.
  • Reply to every message within an hour in the evening. Speed is the close.
